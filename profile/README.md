## Engineering Principles

We design systems around a few core principles:

- **Production-ready** — built for real users, workloads, and operational requirements.
- **Maintainable** — clear project structures, documentation, conventions, and testable components.
- **Scalable** — architectures that can evolve as product and business requirements grow.
- **Secure by design** — authentication, authorization, auditing, secrets handling, isolation, and infrastructure security are considered early.
- **Observable** — logs, metrics, health checks, audit trails, and operational visibility are part of the system.
- **Automation-friendly** — deployments, provisioning, migrations, testing, and repetitive operations should be automated where practical.
- **User-focused** — complex systems should still provide understandable and efficient product experiences.
- **Standards-aware** — systems should account for applicable privacy, security, reliability, and operational requirements.

## How We Work

```text
Discovery
   ↓
Architecture
   ↓
Product & UI Design
   ↓
Development
   ↓
Testing & Hardening
   ↓
Deployment
   ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

### 1. Discovery
Understand the business problem, users, workflows, constraints, and technical requirements.

### 2. Architecture
Define system structure, data models, APIs, infrastructure, security boundaries, and delivery strategy.

### 3. Product & UI Design
Design clear interfaces and workflows around actual user tasks.

### 4. Development
Implement frontend, backend, integrations, databases, automation, and infrastructure.

### 5. Testing & Hardening
Validate functionality, reliability, security, performance, and production readiness.

### 6. Deployment
Configure environments, domains, certificates, monitoring, backups, and deployment automation.

### 7. Continuous Improvement
Maintain, monitor, optimize, and extend the system as requirements evolve.
